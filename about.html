ABOUT CLEAR TRADE LOGISTICS

Your partner in customs
and trade compliance.

Clear Trade Logistics is a South African consultancy specialising in customs clearance, trade compliance and regulatory advisory services.

We work with importers and exporters who need guidance navigating customs procedures, permit applications, documentation and applicable regulatory requirements.

Our approach is centred on understanding the needs of each business, providing clear communication and helping clients manage the complexities of international trade with confidence.

HOW WE WORK

Practical guidance.
Clear communication.

We believe effective trade support starts with understanding your needs and navigating the relevant requirements with care.

  1. 01

    Understand

    We begin by understanding your business, the goods involved and the customs or regulatory requirements relevant to your enquiry.

  2. 02

    Assess

    We review the applicable documentation, permits, classifications and compliance considerations.

  3. 03

    Assist

    We help coordinate the relevant customs clearing, application or regulatory process within the agreed scope of services.

  4. 04

    Communicate

    We aim to maintain clear communication throughout the process, helping you understand the relevant requirements and next steps.
