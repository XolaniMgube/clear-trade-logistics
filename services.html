International trade involves procedures, documentation and regulations that require careful attention.

Clear Trade Logistics provides practical assistance to help businesses understand and navigate the customs and regulatory processes relevant to their operations.

01 / SERVICE

Customs clearance

Efficient customs clearing and documentation support for businesses importing and exporting goods.

We provide customs clearing and documentation assistance for businesses importing or exporting goods.

From tariff classification and declaration preparation to border release coordination, our services help businesses navigate relevant customs procedures and manage their compliance responsibilities.

Included services

  • Import and export customs clearance
  • Customs declaration assistance
  • Preparation of customs documentation
  • Tariff classification support
  • Customs duties and tax guidance
  • Border release coordination
  • Shipment compliance checks
  • Customs process advisory

Clearance timeframes depend on the goods, the documentation and the relevant authorities.

02 / SERVICE

ITAC permits & advisory

Support with import and export permit applications and applicable trade-control requirements.

Certain goods entering or leaving South Africa are subject to import or export controls administered through the International Trade Administration Commission (ITAC).

Clear Trade Logistics assists businesses in understanding applicable permit requirements, preparing supporting documentation and navigating the relevant application processes.

Included services

  • Import permit application assistance
  • Export permit application assistance
  • ITAC regulatory advisory
  • Documentation preparation
  • Applicable trade-control guidance
  • Permit requirement assessments
  • Application coordination and follow-up

Permit approval decisions remain with the relevant regulatory authorities.

03 / SERVICE

SARS trade compliance

Guidance on importer/exporter registrations, customs licensing and ongoing trade compliance.

Maintaining appropriate customs registrations, licences and documentation is an important part of international trade.

Clear Trade Logistics helps businesses understand applicable SARS customs requirements and assists with registration, licensing and related compliance processes.

Included services

  • Importer registration assistance
  • Exporter registration assistance
  • Customs licensing assistance
  • SARS customs regulatory guidance
  • Compliance documentation
  • Trade compliance advisory
  • Registration and licence application support

Registration and licensing decisions are made by SARS.
