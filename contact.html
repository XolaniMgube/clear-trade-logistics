FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers to common questions about our services. For anything else, get in touch.

What services does Clear Trade Logistics provide?

We specialise in customs clearing, ITAC permit application assistance and SARS trade compliance advisory services.

Can you assist with importing goods into South Africa?

We provide customs clearing and regulatory guidance for businesses importing goods into South Africa.

Do all imported products require an ITAC permit?

No. Permit requirements depend on the products involved and the applicable import controls.

We can assist with reviewing the relevant requirements for your goods.

Which industries do you support?

Our focus areas include petroleum products, automotive goods, and solar and energy storage equipment. See Industries We Serve for more detail.

What information should I provide when requesting a consultation?

Provide your contact details, business name, the type of service required and a brief explanation of the goods or trade processes involved.
